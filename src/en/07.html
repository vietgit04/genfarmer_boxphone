Facebook package

Trust and Boost for Facebook: what is in the package, the figures to run it by, and what to watch for.

What is in the package

Facebook is the platform with the most ground covered: Trust rebuilds the account itself, Boost does the seeding.

Trust - growing the account

  • Keeps the profile looking lived-in. Profile picture and cover, bio, interests and occupation, the friend list and auto-adding friends, even the location and activity region - all updated automatically, because an empty profile is what gets checkpointed.
  • AI-powered posting. Posts on a schedule with varied, natural content, shares from pages and groups to generate interaction, mixes content formats, and follows patterns taken from how real users actually post rather than a fixed loop.

Boost - seeding and reach

  • AI auto-seeding for pages and profiles. Reactions and comments on the posts you target, written by AI from the context, with several accounts creating the community effect, and the wording varied so nothing repeats.
  • Auto-join groups by keyword. Finds groups in your field, joins them with secondary accounts, and posts related content into them.
  • Livestream engagement. Raises viewers and reactions during a live, sends hearts, comments in context, and reshares the stream to profiles and groups.
  • Content spinning. Dozens of variants from one base text, so Facebook does not read the posts as spam or duplicate.
  • Auto-sharing to profiles and groups for wider reach.

Instagram package

Trust and Boost for Instagram: what is in the package, the figures to run it by, and what to watch for.

What is in the package

Trust grows the account on real devices; Boost handles publishing and community management across the accounts you own.

Trust - growing the account

  • Login, logout and account management. Many accounts at once, each with its status visible, so the manual slips disappear.
  • Keyword search and AI nurturing. The system looks up content and hashtags around your keywords, the account interacts with what it finds the way a real user would, and it follows at random by keyword or by link.
  • Randomised actions. Browsing the feed, watching reels, liking and commenting on relevant content, with the mix deliberately randomised. The like, comment and view ratios are adjustable per growth phase, and the behaviour is spread across the day to keep checkpoints away.
  • AI posting. Images and captions generated from the topic you choose, which keeps the account active and consistent.

Boost - running the accounts

  • Posts, reels and stories from images you already have, with AI writing the captions, in bulk, on a steady daily schedule.
  • Community management on your own posts. Comments from every account in one place, with AI drafting replies to genuine questions for you to approve, and resharing your posts to stories.
  • Reels at scale. Bulk upload and scheduling, captions and hashtags per market, publishing at the right local time.
  • Profile upkeep. Bios, links and profile details kept consistent across brand accounts, avatars and highlight covers updated in bulk, login health visible on one dashboard, and reel performance tracked across all accounts.

Spotify package

The Spotify package: increasing stream counts, and the checklist to clear before the first run.

What is in the package

Spotify is a single package rather than a Trust and Boost pair: it farms accounts with a real listening history and turns that into plays for the tracks you point it at.

  • Plays around the clock. The system searches out songs and artists at random the way a listener would, varies how long each track is listened to, and builds playlists itself when there are none yet - so the listening history looks like a person's rather than a script's.
  • Songs chosen to fit the pattern. Tracks are picked to match what that account has been listening to, not dropped in at random.
  • Seeding songs and playlists. Liking, listening through to the end, skipping - the ordinary mix of actions, performed automatically.
  • Scale without the pattern. Hundreds of accounts running at once, each on its own device and address, which is what keeps the behaviour from clustering.

What counts as a valid stream

A play only counts if all of these are true, which is why a browser cannot do this job and a phone farm can:

  • Listened to for more than 30 seconds
  • Natural behaviour, no repeated identical actions
  • The device is not rooted and ADB is hidden - see What is Change / SuperChange
  • The IP and network check out - see Router & proxy
  • The account is Premium

Tidal package

The Tidal package: increasing stream counts on a platform the User Manual does not cover yet.

What is in the package

Tidal works the same way as the Spotify package: it produces valid streams for the tracks you point it at.

  • Fully automated: search, click, play, pause, save to library.
  • Listens like a person: random playlists, pauses and skips, and the plays spread across other tracks in the playlist so nothing gets flagged.
  • Managed in bulk: a mini-app drives large numbers of devices in a few clicks.
  • Multi-country: streams can come from several countries through GenRouter.

What counts as a valid stream

  • Listened to for more than 30 seconds
  • Natural behaviour, no repeated identical actions
  • The device is not rooted and ADB is hidden - see What is Change / SuperChange
  • The IP and network check out - see Router & proxy
  • The account is Premium or HiFi

Tidal inspects device fingerprint, OS, network and user behaviour more closely than the other music platforms. That is exactly why it needs a real device farm rather than anything simulated.
