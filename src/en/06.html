2. What the network has to provide

You runDeviceHow the ports are usedSwitch
1 box - 20 phonesGenRouter H3000LAN cable from the modem into the WAN port; your PC into the LAN port, or onto the GENROUTER-2.4GHz / GENROUTER-5GHz Wi-FiNot needed
2 to 4 boxes - 40 to 80 phonesMini PCModem into ETH0; your PC into ETH1, ETH2 or ETH3; the boxes into the remaining portsOnly if you run out of ports
Up to 10 boxes - 200 phonesMini PC + switchModem into ETH0, everything else through the switchYes. The documented stable cluster is 1 PC + 1 mini PC + 1 switch + 10 boxes
More than 10 boxesA second clusterRepeat the cluster aboveYes, one per cluster

GenFarmer's own reference architecture is that cluster of 1 control PC, 1 mini PC, 1 switch and 10 boxes. A switch is recommended from the point where you are running a large number of boxes, both for stability and to spread the load off the mini PC.

  • One free port on the modem for the GenRouter
  • LAN cable: Cat5 or Cat6, Cat6 preferred, to keep transmission stable
  • The control computer does not need Wi-Fi - LAN works just as well, for both the H3000 and the mini PC
  • Minimum bandwidth: around 100 Mbps for the first box and about 100 Mbps more for each box after it. TikTok is the heaviest platform, so that is the figure to plan by. Keep it to a maximum of 5 boxes on one internet line.

In practice the proxy is the bottleneck, not your home line. If the line measures fine and the farm still stutters, suspect the proxies before you pay for more bandwidth.

If you need Wi-Fi and you are on a mini PC

The mini PC connects by LAN only and cannot broadcast Wi-Fi, so USB Boxphones and ordinary phones need a separate device for that. Use an access point - Aruba, Cisco and TP-Link all make them - set to Access Point mode so it only broadcasts, while the router keeps control and hands out the IPs. Ask the seller for a model that can handle your number of connections.

Mini PCConnect over LAN. Wi-Fi does not apply. If your laptop has no Ethernet port, get a USB-to-LAN or Type-C-to-LAN adapter.

Never press the physical reset button on the router unless technical support tells you to, and do not run ipconfig or change the device IP on your computer by hand without guidance. Both are quick ways to lose a working setup.

Put the router in the middle of the room, or as close to the devices as you can. 2.4GHz reaches further but is slower; 5GHz is faster but shorter range. If you replace the charger or cable, use an Android phone charger with a Type-C cable - the router is picky about cables, and an older phone's cable often works when a new one does not.

Once it is wired, the software side is on Connect via USB or Connect via OTG (LAN).

4. Preparing proxies

An account is an identity. A proxy is that identity's home address. If 20 accounts share one address, the platform sees 20 people living in the same house, waking and sleeping at the same time, liking the same kind of content - which is why accounts get banned in clusters rather than one by one.

What follows from that:

  • One proxy per phone. A full box of 20 phones needs 20 proxies
  • For a trial run, 5 accounts over 2-3 proxies is acceptable, but that is not a production setup
  • Isolate Mode is the safety net: when a proxy dies, expires or errors, that phone is cut off from the internet immediately instead of falling back to your real address
  • Besides assigning the proxy, GenRouter also hides WebRTC, DNS, MAC address, location and timezone, blocks traceroute and stops LAN scanning

Which proxies the router accepts

TypeFormat and notes
SOCKS5socks5://user:pass@ip:port
HTTP / HTTPSSupported, same workflow
PPPoEBuilds proxies from a multi-session line. Vietnam only - the number of proxies equals the number of sessions on the line, and the IPs come from a real ISP

Adding them

  1. Prepare the list first, in Notepad or a document, one proxy per line
  2. Open 192.168.5.1:9000, go to Proxy Manager, click Add proxy
  3. Pick the format that matches your list, paste it in, click Add proxy
  4. To assign: Devices → Update proxy, choose the protocol, then either type the details (Input Manually) or pick one already stored (Select from List)
  5. To do the whole box at once, tick the checkbox at the top of the device table, click Update Proxy and paste the list - the router hands a different proxy to each device

Buying advice - which provider, residential against datacenter, and whether rotating proxies are safe on a farm - is the one part still open. Ask support rather than guessing. If you are in Vietnam, the multi-session PPPoE route means you may not need to buy proxies at all.

Why a phone farm rather than an antidetect browser

Social and music platforms have stopped being workable from an antidetect browser: a browser leaks its pattern, checkpoints come fast, engagement gets suppressed, and a browser cannot produce a listening environment that counts as a real stream. Real devices on real IPs are what the packages are built for.

The four steps every package follows

Warm the account up → Set its audience → Trigger engagement → Push distribution

Nothing gets pushed on day one, and nothing is spammed. The whole sequence is timed the way a real person behaves.

What the algorithms actually measure

The same five signals decide reach on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, which is why the packages are shaped the way they are.

SignalWhat it means for you
Watch time and retentionThe most important one: did the viewer stop, watch to the end, rewatch, or leave early
Early engagementLikes, shares, comments and saves have to appear soon after posting. Dumped all at once looks wrong and gets suppressed; spread out naturally, the data flows in evenly
Who is engagingNot just how many, but whether they match the content. The wrong audience teaches the algorithm the wrong thing and the distribution goes to the wrong people
The account's historyAn account that has run junk engagement is very hard to recover. Starting correctly matters more than running hard
Non-follower responseThe platform is asking whether new people react, not whether your followers do. A post that wakes up non-followers spreads fast even from a small account

Auto Login sits in front of both: it logs the accounts in and out in bulk instead of you typing on each phone.

Turning on Boost for an account that has not been warmed up is the fastest way to lose it, and an account that has once run junk engagement is very hard to recover. This is the most expensive mistake in the whole roadmap.

The step-by-step version of running these is on Day 3 - Basic automation.

What keeps it looking human

  • Randomised tasks: the timing and the type of action - view, like, save, scroll - change from run to run instead of repeating a fixed pattern
  • Limits and rest: the scripts have ceilings, pauses and background behaviour (watch, scroll, skip past), which is what the platform reads as natural
  • Built per platform: each package is shaped around how that platform distributes content and how its users behave in your market

Before you run your first package

  • The 5 trial accounts have survived the 48-hour hands-off period without a verification prompt - see Day 2
  • Every phone has its own proxy, and Isolate Mode is on - see Router & proxy
  • The platform app is installed on every phone, from the official APK folder rather than the links in the User Manual - see Day 2 · step 3
  • You have a vilao.ai API key if the package comments automatically - this was part of Day 0, and finding out halfway through that you do not have one means stopping halfway

TikTok package

Trust and Boost for TikTok: what is in the package, the figures to run it by, and what to watch for.

What is in the package

TikTok has both halves of the set. Trust builds an account with a real activity history so it stops being treated as a throwaway; Boost runs the publishing side across the accounts you own.

Trust - growing the account

  • Grow by industry keywords. The system searches out videos around your target keywords and behaves like a real person browsing them, so the account starts being recommended into those keywords and builds a history that makes it look trustworthy.
  • Auto-post and comment with AI. Posting runs on a schedule, and the comments are generated rather than copied, so a large number of accounts never end up writing the same line.
  • Manage many accounts at once. Hundreds of accounts on one screen, synchronised and monitored centrally, with no technical skill required.
  • Random follows. The system picks from the suggested list and follows at random, so the behaviour never looks like a repeated action.

Boost - running the channels

  • Contextual AI replies. AI reads the comments left on your own videos and drafts replies that match the topic and the tone, phrased differently every time.
  • Schedule and publish at scale. Upload and schedule videos in bulk across your account list, publish at the right time for each market, and stagger the publishing so channels are not posting identically.
  • One interface for every account, with per-channel reporting instead of numbers scattered across logins.

Because it all runs on real handsets, the uploads happen in a native mobile environment rather than a browser.

Why change the information tied to the link every week or two. Change it every day and nobody keeps up; leave it untouched for months and you are hammering the same list of links over and over, which is exactly the pattern the platforms sweep for.

Notes specific to TikTok

  • TikTok is the heaviest platform for bandwidth, which is why the 100 Mbps per box figure is based on it.
  • The APK links for TikTok in the User Manual are the only ones that are correct, but the official folder in Day 2 · step 3 is still the safer source.

Signs that mean stop

  1. Accounts are asked for verification more often than usual.
  2. Posts suddenly lose all their views.
  3. The number of running phones keeps dropping for no clear reason.

If any account is banned overnight, do not scale up. Go back and check the proxies and the running speed first.
